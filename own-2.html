Our Story
from strangers to forever

Our story started in the simplest way possible. ❤️

We first saw each other during the very first week of our first year in college. At that time, neither of us knew that this random meeting would eventually become such a beautiful part of our lives. Just a week later, I accepted you, and somehow, that little moment became the beginning of our story. ❤️

From our first year of college until our fourth year, we went through so much together. We explored new places, made countless memories, laughed over the smallest things, and slowly learned so much about each other.

Of course, our journey was never perfect. We had so many fights, misunderstandings, emotional moments, and ups and downs. There were times when we hurt each other, but we also healed together. We learned from our mistakes, and somehow always found our way back to each other. ❤️

Now college is over, and we have stepped into our working lives, but our story is still continuing. Life has changed, our responsibilities have changed, and we have both grown so much, but somehow, we are still choosing each other.

I know I have made so many mistakes throughout this journey. I know there were times when I hurt you, misunderstood you, or made things more difficult. But you never left me. No matter how many ups and downs we went through, you stayed. You cared. You came back. You kept choosing us. ❤️

And I think that is what makes our story so special.

We didn't just fall in love.
We grew together.
We explored together.
We fought together.
We hurt each other and healed together.
We made mistakes together.
We learned together.
We changed together.
And after everything, we still chose each other. ❤️

From two strangers who met during the first week of college to two people who are still holding on to each other after all these years...

Maybe our story was never perfect, but it has always been ours. ❤️
